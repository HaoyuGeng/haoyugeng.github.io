About me

Hi! I’m Haoyu Geng, a postdoctoral researcher in the School of Statistics and Data Science at Nankai University, advised by Professor Changliang Zou. I received my B.S. and Ph.D. in Statistics from Nankai University in 2020 and 2025, respectively. My Ph.D. advisors were Professors Changliang Zou and Zhaojun Wang.

My research focuses on high-dimensional inference, changepoint analysis, and extreme value theory. I develop data-driven methods to characterize differences across populations, environments, and time, while using shared information to support reliable inference and uncertainty quantification. I am also interested in connecting heterogeneity analysis, predictive risk assessment, and decision optimization for AI systems operating in changing environments.
